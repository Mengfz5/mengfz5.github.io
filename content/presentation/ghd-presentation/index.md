## Scope

This guided refresher reconstructs the bridge from microscopic current fluctuations to hydrodynamic diffusion in De Nardis, Bernard and Doyon, *Diffusion in generalized hydrodynamics and quasiparticle scattering*, SciPost Phys. **6**, 049 (2019).

The deck covers the introduction, sections 2.1–2.5, the response argument of Appendix B, selected supporting arguments from Appendix C, and the opening of section 3.1 through dressing, Eq. (3.9). It does not derive the microscopic diffusion formula of section 4.

- **Slides 1–26:** the main argument, from propagation versus broadening to conservation, closure, correlations, transport coefficients, density conventions, PT symmetry and quasiparticle dressing.
- **Slides 27–34:** supporting derivations and source-audit qualifications for discussion.
- **Slide 35:** sources and coverage.

Packet sketches and scalar checks are teaching illustrations, not simulated or experimentally measured model data. Apparent source discrepancies are labelled as such, not as author-confirmed errata.
